# Intervention Pack — 12 F1 pedagogical cells



The scientific target for a given transition is fixed across all three treatments. Tutor examples are **generated instructional instances**, separate from assessment instances. No compulsory historical bit string or scripted progression.



## P1 Worked Example — MODEL

Present a complete scientifically correct solution and **why** its inference is warranted, within the target SOLO level. The modeled reasoning is the primary learning mechanism; short engagement questions do not convert it to Guided Questioning.



## P2 Guided Questioning — GUIDE

Elicit learner ideas with bounded, responsive prompts. If a learner provides a correct conclusion without reason, probe the mechanism appropriate to the transition; if wrong, provide minimal corrective information and targeted follow-up, without turning the interaction into a full worked solution.



## P3 Contrasting Cases — COMPARE

Construct two verified cases differing in a diagnostically important relation while keeping other relevant variables comparable. Have the learner explain what changes and why. Merely presenting two unrelated examples is not contrasting-case pedagogy.



## Shared treatment-fidelity rules

Use equal opportunity budgets under approved protocol; no assessment questions/keys in Tutor context. Do not infer completion from turn counter or model self-report. Assess actual displayed behavior and transcript. Scenario generation must use F1 rules but not copy frozen assessment instances.

### C1-P1

**Mechanism:** Worked Example.

**Scientific objective:** Establish one usable relationship between sender-selected check bit and receiver-complete-frame test.

**Target knowledge:** K1, K2, K3, R1

**Suggested learning trajectory (non-scripted):** name agreed even-parity rule; model parity-bit choice; show complete-frame check and mismatch; invite simple application

**Instructional boundary:** Do not teach multi-flip miss laws or 2D location.

**Treatment fidelity:** Inspect participant transcript to confirm Tutor explicitly MODELS the target organization in a fully resolved instructional example, including the why and the boundary of the conclusion.

**Learner contingency:** Correct-without-reason → level-appropriate probing; materially incorrect → verified bounded diagnostic case; sufficient → consolidate. All within the assigned treatment.
